Syrex Platform
Data Glossary & Key Concepts

Definitions for every important entity, number format, and concept used across the platform. Use this as a reference when reading reports, flowcharts, or API responses.

Version 1.0  ·  Syrex Engineering  ·  2026

Document Numbering Formats

All document numbers in Syrex are system-generated, sequential, and unique per year. They are never reused, never manually editable, and always displayed in a consistent format.

Sales Order SO-YYYY-######
Example: SO-2026-000001

A unique identifier for every sales order. The year resets the counter — so SO-2025-000999 and SO-2026-000001 are different orders from different years.
Invoice INV-YYYY-######
Example: INV-2026-000001

One invoice is generated per approved order (unless it is a warranty replacement order, which has no invoice). Invoice numbers are separate from order numbers — they have their own sequence.
Service Complaint CMP-YYYY-######
Example: CMP-2026-000001

Unique identifier for every after-sales service complaint. Referenced in all communication with service engineers and warranty decisions.

Core Entities

Outlet
A trade outlet is a dealer, retailer, or distributor who buys from you. Every outlet has:
  • A unique outlet code
  • An assigned warehouse (determines which warehouse fulfils their orders)
  • A real-time outstanding balance (sum of all unpaid invoice amounts)
  • Contact details and delivery address
Warehouse
A physical storage and dispatch location. Each warehouse has its own stock levels per product. Outlet orders are always fulfilled from the outlet's assigned warehouse. A warehouse manager can only see and dispatch from their own warehouse.
Sales Order
A formal request from an outlet to purchase products. Contains one or more order lines (product + quantity + unit price). Triggers the entire downstream flow: approval → invoice → dispatch.
Order Line
A single product entry within an order. Tracks:
  • qtyOrdered — what was requested
  • qtyDispatched — what has been shipped so far
  • status — pending / partially_dispatched / fully_dispatched
Partial dispatch is allowed — you can ship 50 units today and 50 next week.
Dispatch
A physical shipment from warehouse to outlet. One dispatch can cover lines from multiple orders. Records:
  • Transporter name and vehicle number
  • LR / GR number (lorry receipt)
  • Serial numbers per dispatched unit
  • Status: created on dispatch
Invoice
A billing document auto-created when an order is approved. Contains:
  • Line items (product, qty, unit price, line total)
  • Tax and charge rows (GST, freight, etc.)
  • Subtotal, total, amount paid, amount due
Amount due is updated in real time as payments are received.
Payment
A cash, cheque, or bank transfer received from an outlet. Payments are automatically allocated to the outlet's oldest unpaid invoices first (FIFO). Each payment stores a UTR / reference number for reconciliation.
Outstanding Balance
The total unpaid invoice amount for an outlet at any given moment. Automatically recalculated after every new invoice and every payment. Visible on the outlet profile and in all AR reports.
Tax / Charge
A configurable line added to invoices. Can be:
  • Percentage — e.g. 18% GST on subtotal
  • Fixed — e.g. ₹250 handling charge
The rate card is centrally managed. Changes apply to new invoices only.

Service & Warranty Concepts

Serial Number
A unique identifier printed or stickered on a physical unit. Captured at dispatch time. Used as the primary key for warranty lookups and complaint logging. The system normalises serials (uppercase, alphanumeric only) to prevent mismatch from formatting differences.
Service Complaint
A formal after-sales issue raised against one or more serial numbers. Tracks the complaint from first report through site visit, testing, and resolution or warranty replacement. Every complaint has a complete immutable activity log.
ASI
Area Service Inspector — the regional manager responsible for overseeing service complaints in a territory. Assigned to complaints alongside a Service Engineer.
Service Engineer (SE)
The field technician who visits the outlet site, performs tests, and submits test reports. Their findings drive the warranty decision.
Warranty Decision
A formal judgement made after a test report is submitted:
  • Approved — unit is defective; a replacement will be sent
  • Rejected — no manufacturing defect; complaint closed
Only users with the service:approve permission can make this decision.
Replacement Order
A sales order auto-created after warranty approval. It is:
  • Type: warranty_replacement
  • Pre-approved (no manual approval needed)
  • Zero value (no invoice generated)
  • Flows through the normal dispatch pipeline
Telephonic Closure
A complaint resolved over a phone call, without requiring a site visit. A mandatory reason note is recorded. Used for minor issues that can be guided to resolution remotely.
Serial Event Ledger
A per-serial timeline of every significant event: first dispatch, complaints raised, and replacement assignment. This gives a complete ownership and warranty history for any unit — useful for audits, disputes, and repeat failures.

Field Sense Concepts

Shift
A working day for a field sales agent. Opens automatically (cron) when the agent's schedule says they should be working, and closes automatically at 13:30 UTC (19:00 IST). GPS tracking is active during an open shift.
Shift Schedule
A configuration that tells the system which days and times a particular agent is expected to work. Used by the auto-start cron to open shifts at the right time.
Field Visit
A logged arrival and departure at a specific outlet by a field agent during their shift. Timestamps and location coordinates are captured. Managers can see visit history per agent and per outlet.
GPS Trail
The sequence of GPS coordinates captured during a shift. The platform applies RDP (Ramer-Douglas-Peucker) simplification before storage to remove redundant intermediate points, keeping storage efficient while preserving route accuracy.
SSE Live Stream
Server-Sent Events — a technology that pushes real-time GPS updates from the backend to the admin dashboard map without the browser needing to poll. Updates appear instantly as agents move.

Access & Security Concepts

Permission
A granular capability flag in the format resource:action, e.g.:
  • orders:read — view orders
  • orders:write — create orders
  • orders:manage — approve/hold/reject orders
  • orders:approve — specifically approve (separate from manage)
Permissions are grouped into roles. The wildcard * grants everything.
Role
A named bundle of permissions assigned to a user. Roles are fully customisable — you can create any role with any combination of permissions. A user has exactly one role.
Audit Log
An append-only record of every significant action: who did it, when, what entity was affected, and what changed. Used for compliance, dispute resolution, and accountability. Cannot be edited or deleted.

Warehouse Scoping: A warehouse manager can only see outlets, orders, and dispatches linked to their assigned warehouse. They cannot see data from other warehouses — even if they have the read permission. This scoping is enforced at the API level, not just the UI.
